What these mean
Faithfulness — how much of the answer is supported by the retrieved sources. Higher is better.
Hallucination — 1 − faithfulness: the share of the answer not grounded in sources. Lower is better.
Answer relevancy — how directly the answer addresses the question.
Context precision — of the sources retrieved, how many were the expected ones.
Context recall — of the expected sources, how many were retrieved.
Engines — Lexical is fast & free (token overlap); LLM judge uses Azure OpenAI for entailment-based scoring.
